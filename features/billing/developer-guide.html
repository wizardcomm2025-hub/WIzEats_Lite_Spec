Feature developer guide · BILL

Bill & payment

Saving an order's bill with its number, taking cash / UPI / split payment, and reading a saved bill.

v1 scope

Three use cases: generate the bill, take payment, get a saved bill. See the README. The spec records today's behaviour plus targets (re-synced 2026-11-24; no billing server code changed): BILL-D2 is fixed in the app; BILL-D1 and BILL-D3 are To fix.

How it works end-to-end

The app's Bill Payment page prices the order and posts orders/generateBill/{id} with eight totals, then payments/create2 with the legs. The server stores the totals as sent, snapshots the lines into bill_items, numbers the bill per financial year, and on payment closes the order, marks the bill paid and frees the table or room.

What matters where you sit

POS API

  • All totals and payment amounts come from the client; nothing is reconciled (BILL-D1). Target: the server works the bill out from the lines, the order's tax % and the discount (the app's rules), and the legs must add up to the grand total.
  • payments.*.amount is validated but not stored; paymentType is free text. Target: stored (new column), CASH / UPI only.
  • Payment errors are always HTTP 500, with exception text for non-database errors (BILL-D3). Target: 404 / 409 / 422 and no exception text; owner and waiter only; Idempotency-Key honoured.
  • payments/bill/{id} converts the ID outside its try (target 404 — BILL-D3).

Data — MySQL

  • Bill numbers: max + 1 per restaurant and financial year, serialised by locking restaurant_configs; no unique key (target: unique key — BILL-D3).
  • bill_items.menuItemName is NOT NULL, so orders with an unnamed line can't be billed.
  • Generating a bill overwrites the order's subTotal with the posted one.

POS app

  • The bill pages open from the cart (View Bill, Collect Payment) and Order History → Live (BILL-D2 fixed with ORD-D2).
  • It bills first, checks the saved total equals its own, then pays all legs in one request. useCustomerDisplay mirrors the total and the change on terminals with a customer display; Bill Breakdown plays a Discount Applied! animation.

Super-admin panel

None.

Integrations

None. UPI is only recorded.

Accepted risks (do not "fix" without a new decision)
