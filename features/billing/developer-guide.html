Feature developer guide · BILL

Bill & payment

Saving an order's bill with its number, taking cash / UPI / split payment, and reading a saved bill.

v1 scope

Three use cases: generate the bill, take payment, get a saved bill. See the README. The spec records today's behaviour; BILL-D1 to D3 keep the current rules as accepted risks.

How it works end-to-end

The app's Bill Payment page prices the order and posts orders/generateBill/{id} with eight totals, then payments/create2 with the legs. The server stores the totals as sent, snapshots the lines into bill_items, numbers the bill per financial year, and on payment closes the order, marks the bill paid and frees the table or room.

What matters where you sit

POS API

  • All totals and payment amounts come from the client; nothing is reconciled (BILL-D1).
  • payments.*.amount is validated but not stored; paymentType is free text.
  • Payment errors are always HTTP 500, with exception text for non-database errors (BILL-D3).
  • payments/bill/{id} converts the ID outside its try (BILL-D2).

Data — MySQL

  • Bill numbers: max + 1 per restaurant and financial year, serialised by locking restaurant_configs; no unique key.
  • bill_items.menuItemName is NOT NULL, so orders with an unnamed line can't be billed.
  • Generating a bill overwrites the order's subTotal with the posted one.

POS app

  • The bill page is unreachable: Number(hex) in the cart, and getAllOrders failing from Order History (BILL-D2, ORD-D2).
  • Once reachable, it bills first, checks the saved total equals its own, then pays all legs in one request.

Super-admin panel

None.

Integrations

None. UPI is only recorded.

Accepted risks (do not "fix" without a new decision)

  • BILL-D1: client-supplied totals and amounts; unnamed lines block billing.
  • BILL-D2: app can't bill or pay; tables of app orders not freed; bare 500 on bad bill ID.
  • BILL-D3: any role; no void/refund; numbering lock; exception text in errors.
